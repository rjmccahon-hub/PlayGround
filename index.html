How to Play

⛏ Mine & Build

Hold left-click to break a block and collect it. Right-click places the selected hotbar block. Aim with the crosshair. You can build anywhere you can reach.

☁ Get Around

WASD to move, Space to jump, Shift to sprint. You can climb one-block steps automatically. Leap the floating stones east of the island to find the sky beacon.

✦ Help the Villagers

Walk up to a villager and press E. Accept their quest, gather what they need, then return to claim coins, XP, and rare blocks. Hats unlock as rewards.

◈ Spend & Style

Open the shop with B to buy blocks with coins. Open the wardrobe with C to change your shirt, hair, and unlocked hats. Press I for your full inventory.

♛ Build a Kingdom

Your edits persist in this browser. Try a tower on the east shore, a bridge between platforms, or a cozy hut by the pond. There is no wrong block.

📱 Touch Controls

On a phone or tablet, use the left joystick to move, drag the world to look, and tap the action buttons to jump, mine, place, and talk.
